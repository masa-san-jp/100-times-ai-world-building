# World Reference

## Overview

### [Thalassia](entities/e1.md)

Thalassia is a stilt-built river city situated above a delta subject to annual flooding. The physical layout dictates that infrastructure must withstand seasonal water rise, constraining construction and labor cycles. The city's hydraulic rhythm is measured in FlwYr units, with specific markers Flw, Drn, and Slt tracking the flood phases. This geographic reality underpins the DredgeTriumvirate, CordKeepers, and StiltWard, who manage the necessary channel maintenance and structural integrity against the recurring environmental pressure.

### [DredgeTriumvirate](entities/e7.md)

The DredgeTriumvirate is the institutional framework governing channel maintenance in Thalassia, established to manage the annual delta flood cycle (FlwYr) that threatens stilt infrastructure. This tripartite structure allocates DredgeRight among three guilds to enforce a sediment removal quota of 120 DdgL per member per FlwYr. By binding labor obligations and record-keeping via CrdKnot to specific calendar markers (Flw, Drn, Slt), the institution ensures hydraulic stability and prevents infrastructure collapse through regulated, rotating maintenance efforts.

### [StiltDredgers](entities/e16.md)

StiltDredgers are laborers in Thalassia responsible for executing the 120 DdgL sediment removal quota per FlwYr. Their work is governed by the DredgeTriumvirate and verified through CrdKnot records managed by CordKeepers. They operate within the constraints of the annual delta flood, ensuring channel integrity to maintain the stilt infrastructure. Their duties rotate based on the FloodCount cycle, specifically during Flw and Drn markers, to balance labor distribution among the three guilds.
