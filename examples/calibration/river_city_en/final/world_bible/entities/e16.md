## Summary

StiltDredgers are laborers in Thalassia responsible for executing the 120 DdgL sediment removal quota per FlwYr. Their work is governed by the DredgeTriumvirate and verified through CrdKnot records managed by CordKeepers. They operate within the constraints of the annual delta flood, ensuring channel integrity to maintain the stilt infrastructure. Their duties rotate based on the FloodCount cycle, specifically during Flw and Drn markers, to balance labor distribution among the three guilds.
