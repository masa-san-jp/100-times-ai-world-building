## Summary

The Delta Stilt Region is the hydrological and structural foundation of Thalassia, characterized by a network of vertical pilings anchored in sedimentary soil. This region operates under the annual FlwYr cycle, where rising water levels necessitate the maintenance of channel integrity by the DredgeTriumvirate. Local regulations limit sediment extraction to 120 DdgL per cycle to prevent destabilizing the stilt base.
