## Summary

CordAnchors are the operational subset of PilingTenders within Thalassia responsible for verifying stilt integrity via CrdKnot during the Flw marker. Unlike StiltDredgers who manage 120 DdgL sediment quotas, CordAnchors focus on structural tension. Their labor is logged in 3 FlwCrd segments, distinct from the broader DredgeTriumvirate agreements. They operate under StiltWard oversight, ensuring pilings withstand hydraulic pressure while maintaining archive accuracy through knotted cord protocols.
