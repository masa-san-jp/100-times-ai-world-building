## Summary

The Verdant Dredge Compact is the administrative framework enforcing the 120 DdgL sediment removal quota per FlwYr in e2. It formalizes the shared DredgeRight among the three guilds, structuring labor rotation and CrdKnot record-keeping to ensure channel maintenance aligns with the hydraulic limits of the delta. This institution binds the political authority of the DredgeTriumvirate to the physical constraints of the stilt city, standardizing the 3 FlwCrd record segments used to verify compliance.

## Facts

- [number] The standard record segment length for quota verification under the Verdant Dredge Compact is 3 FlwCrd.
- [proper noun] TidalGauge is the instrument used by Verdant Dredge Compact to measure the 120 DdgL quota per FlwYr.
