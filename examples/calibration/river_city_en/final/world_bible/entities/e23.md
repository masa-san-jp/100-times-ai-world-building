## Summary

KnotBound designates the labor class within Thalassia bound to the 120 DdgL sediment removal quota per FlwYr. This group executes the physical dredging tasks governed by the DredgeTriumvirate and StiltWard, operating under the law_norms of the QuotaCodex. Their obligations are verified through CrdKnot records managed by CordKeepers, linking their manual labor to the structural integrity of the stilt infrastructure during the Flw marker of the FloodCount cycle.

## Facts

- [number] CrdKnot verification segment length per KnotBound obligation is 3 FlwCrd.
- [proper noun] SiltKnots are the CrdKnot verification marks applied by CordKeepers to confirm KnotBound labor completion.
