## Summary

CordQuotaGuild members operate at Gullspire (e4) by translating the 120 DdgL sediment quota into CrdKnot sequences. This labor binds DredgeRight obligations to physical record segments, measured in FlwCrd. The guild’s practice distinguishes hydraulic maintenance from archival verification, ensuring that each FlwYr cycle’s work is encoded in knotted cords that define the group’s standing within the DredgeTriumvirate structure.
