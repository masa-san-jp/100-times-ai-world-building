## Summary

Gullspire is the central stilt platform within Verdant, serving as the operational hub for the DredgeTriumvirate. The structure is anchored by vertical pilings in delta sediment, designed to withstand the annual FlwYr flood cycle. Guild laborers execute the 120 DdgL sediment removal quota using standard tools, while the StiltWard monitors structural integrity. Records of these operations are maintained by the CordKeepers using knotted cords, with each segment measuring 3 FlwCrd.
