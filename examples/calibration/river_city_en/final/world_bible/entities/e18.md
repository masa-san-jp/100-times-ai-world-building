## Summary

QuotaBound denotes the labor obligation binding StiltDredgers and PilingTenders to the 120 DdgL sediment removal quota per FlwYr. This status is verified through CrdKnot records under the DredgeTriumvirate and CordKeepers, intersecting with StiltWard structural mandates. It defines the legal and physical constraints of work within Thalassia, linking ecological maintenance to institutional authority and individual duty during the annual flood cycle.
