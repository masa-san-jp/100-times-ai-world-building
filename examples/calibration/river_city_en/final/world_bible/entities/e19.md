## Summary

CordBound denotes the labor status of StiltDredgers and PilingTenders within Thalassia, binding them to a 120 DdgL sediment removal quota per FlwYr. This obligation is verified via CrdKnot records by CordKeepers, intersecting with StiltWard maintenance duties during the Flw marker. The status ensures structural integrity and channel clearance, governed by DredgeTriumvirate norms.

## Facts

- [number] Sediment removal quota per CordBound laborer is 120 DdgL.
- [proper noun] ThreadObligate is the CrdKnot verification protocol binding CordBound laborers to the 120 DdgL quota.
