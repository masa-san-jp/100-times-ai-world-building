## Summary

PilingTenders maintain the structural integrity of Thalassia's vertical pilings, particularly during the Flw marker of the FloodCount cycle. They secure anchoring points against hydraulic pressure, ensuring the stilt infrastructure remains stable despite annual delta flooding. Their work is verified through CrdKnot records managed by the CordKeepers, linking physical maintenance to the DredgeTriumvirate's regulatory framework. This role distinguishes them from dredging laborers by focusing on structural preservation rather than sediment removal.
