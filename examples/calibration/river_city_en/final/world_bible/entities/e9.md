## Summary

PilingBench District is the operational core of Gullspire, housing the DredgeTriumvirate's sediment processing stations. It functions as the primary site for measuring 120 DdgL quotas per FlwYr using DredgeScoop instruments. The district's infrastructure is anchored by vertical pilings in delta sediment, designed to maintain stability during the annual FlwYr flood cycle. CordKeepers manage the CrdKnot archives here, ensuring record integrity via 3 FlwCrd standard segments. StiltWard personnel oversee structural integrity, limiting archive access during the Drn and Slt markers of the FloodCount
