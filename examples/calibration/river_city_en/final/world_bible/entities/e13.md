## Summary

CordTenders are the operational laborers within the CordKeepers guild who maintain the physical integrity of the CrdKnot archive. They manage the storage of FlwCrdCord segments on the CordShelf, ensuring that each record adheres to the 3 FlwCrd standard length. Their daily routine involves inspecting knot density and cord tension to prevent data loss, particularly during the FlwYr cycle when humidity from the delta affects material stability. Unlike the StiltWard, who restrict access, CordTenders execute the CrdKnot procedure, tying entries into the KnotLedger registry to preserve the DredgeT

## Facts

- [number] Standard FlwCrdCord segment length managed by CordTenders is 3 FlwCrd.
- [proper noun] KnotTether is the standard fastening tool used by CordTenders to secure FlwCrdCord segments at the CordShelf.
- [object] CrdKnotCord is the standard cord material processed by CordTenders at the CordShelf.
- [procedure] CordTenders Tie CrdKnot entries into the KnotLedger registry at the CordShelf during the Drn phase of each FloodCount cycle.
