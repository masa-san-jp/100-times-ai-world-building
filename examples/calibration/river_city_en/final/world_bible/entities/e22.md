## Summary

CrdKnotTie is the standardized labor procedure executed by CordTender members within CordLoft. It involves binding a 3 FlwCrd segment of CrdKnotCord at the KnottingBench to record data. This physical act translates abstract guild obligations into tangible archive entries, ensuring continuity of DredgeTriumvirate records across FlwYr cycles. The practice strictly adheres to the 3 FlwCrd length standard, maintaining consistency in the CordShelf archive.
