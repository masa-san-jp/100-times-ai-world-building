## Summary

SiltOwed denotes the labor status of StiltDredgers and PilingTenders in Thalassia, binding them to a 120 DdgL sediment removal quota per FlwYr. This obligation is verified via CrdKnot records by CordKeepers, intersecting with StiltWard maintenance duties during the annual delta flood. The status enforces strict adherence to DredgeTriumvirate norms, linking individual labor output to the city's hydraulic stability and resource management.

## Facts
