## Summary

CordTender comprises the labor segment within CordLoft responsible for maintaining CrdKnot archives on the CordShelf. Operating under CordKeepers authority, they execute the physical knotting of FlwCrdCord units to a standard 3 FlwCrd length. This role is distinct from the DredgeTriumvirate's channel work, focusing instead on the integrity of records that track DdgL quotas across the FloodCount cycle.

## Facts

- [number] CordTender knotting length per CrdKnot entry is 3 FlwCrd.
- [proper noun] KnotBinder is the designated role title for CordTender members performing CrdKnot entries at CordShelf.
- [object] KnottingBench is the physical workstation at CordShelf where CordTender members execute CrdKnot entries using FlwCrdCord units.
- [procedure] CordTender ties 3 FlwCrd knot on FlwCrdCord at KnottingBench.
