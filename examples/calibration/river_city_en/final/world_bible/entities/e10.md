## Summary

CordShelf is the primary storage site within CordLoft (e6), housing knotted cords measured in FlwCrd for CrdKnot records. It serves the CordKeepers by organizing archive segments, with standard record cord lengths defined as 3 FlwCrd. The site facilitates the maintenance of DredgeTriumvirate coordination documents, ensuring data integrity across the FlwYr cycle. Access is regulated to preserve the structural integrity of the stilt platform during annual flooding events.

## Facts

- [object] FlwCrdCord is the standard knotted cord unit stored on CordShelf, measured in FlwCrd.
- [procedure] CordKeeper ties 3 FlwCrd knot on FlwCrdCord at CordShelf.
