## Summary

CordLoft is the administrative district of Gullspire dedicated to CrdKnot record-keeping and DredgeTriumvirate coordination. It houses the CordKeepers and features a vertical archive of knotted cords measured in FlwCrd. The district enforces the 120 DdgL sediment quota per FlwYr and serves as the primary interface for FlwRead data verification during the Drn marker period.

## Facts

- [number] The standard record cord segment length in the CordLoft archive is 3 FlwCrd.
- [proper noun] KnotShelf is the primary storage unit within CordLoft, holding knotted cords measured in FlwCrd for CrdKnot records.
- [object] FlwCrdCord is the standard knotted cord unit used in CordLoft for CrdKnot records, measured in FlwCrd.
