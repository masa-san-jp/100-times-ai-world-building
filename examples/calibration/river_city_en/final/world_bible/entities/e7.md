## Summary

The DredgeTriumvirate is the institutional framework governing channel maintenance in Thalassia, established to manage the annual delta flood cycle (FlwYr) that threatens stilt infrastructure. This tripartite structure allocates DredgeRight among three guilds to enforce a sediment removal quota of 120 DdgL per member per FlwYr. By binding labor obligations and record-keeping via CrdKnot to specific calendar markers (Flw, Drn, Slt), the institution ensures hydraulic stability and prevents infrastructure collapse through regulated, rotating maintenance efforts.
