## Summary

TideQuotaLifters are the labor cohort within the DredgeTriumvirate responsible for executing the 120 DdgL sediment removal quota per FlwYr. They operate in the Delta Stilt Region, where annual flooding dictates the operational rhythm of the Flw marker. Their duties are verified via CrdKnot records managed by CordKeepers, ensuring compliance with the shared rights of the three guilds. This group provides the physical labor necessary to maintain channel integrity, distinct from the structural focus of PilingTenders.
