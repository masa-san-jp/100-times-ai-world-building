## Summary

CordWrights are the laborers within the DredgeTriumvirate who execute the 120 DdgL sediment removal quota per FlwYr in Verdant. Their work is governed by law_norms that bind CrdKnot obligations, requiring them to physically knot record cords in 3 FlwCrd segments to log DdgL volumes. This practice links their physical labor in the delta channels directly to the administrative archive, ensuring that the physical act of dredging is mirrored in the linguistic system of knotted records. Their role is distinct from the StiltWard, focusing on channel maintenance rather than structural integrity.
