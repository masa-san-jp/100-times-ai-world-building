## Summary

FirstFlwRecord marks the initial documented annual delta flood, establishing the FloodCount calendar baseline. This event necessitated stilt construction for the river city and formalized the DredgeTriumvirate's shared DredgeRight. It introduced CrdKnot as the standard recording method, replacing oral tradition with physical cord segments measured in FlwCrd. The hydraulic constraints of the delta defined the 120 DdgL sediment removal quota per FlwYr, binding labor obligations to specific Flw, Drn, and Slt markers. This historical layer explains the current infrastructure limits and the CordWrd
