## Summary

Verdant is the eastern channel district of Thalassia, situated above the delta where annual flooding dictates infrastructure limits. Managed by the DredgeTriumvirate, it enforces a 120 DdgL sediment removal quota per FlwYr. Records are maintained on knotted cords, with the StiltWard overseeing structural integrity and the CordKeepers managing archive access. FloodCount cycles structure labor rotations and hydraulic maintenance routines within this region.
